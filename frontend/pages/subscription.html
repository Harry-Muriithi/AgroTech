Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my free trial ends? ▾
Your account and all your data (scans, schedule, inventory, profit records) stay safe. You will need to subscribe to a plan to continue scanning crops and using AI features.
How is Small Scale vs Large Scale decided? ▾
It's based on the farm size you entered when registering. Under 5 acres = Small Scale. 5 acres or more = Large Scale. You can update your farm size in your Profile.
Can I switch plans later? ▾
Yes. If your farm size changes, update it in your Profile page and your plan classification will update automatically.
What payment methods will be accepted? ▾
M-Pesa Paybill will be the primary payment method, designed for Kenyan farmers. More details will be shared before your trial ends.
What counts as a "scan"? ▾
Each time you upload or take a photo of a crop leaf and the AI analyses it, that counts as one scan. Small Scale farmers get 100 scans per day; Large Scale farmers get unlimited scans.
